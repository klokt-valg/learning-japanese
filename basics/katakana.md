## Voiced Consonants (Dakuon だくおん & Handakuon はんだくおん)

### Dakuon (だくおん)

You can add a small tenten (゛, two short dashes) to certain Katakana characters to "voice" their consonant. This changes a hard, unvoiced sound (like 'k') into a softer, voiced sound (like 'g').
