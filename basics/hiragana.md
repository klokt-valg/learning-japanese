## Voiced Consonants(Dakuon だくおん & Handakuon はんだくおん)

### Dakuon(だくおん)

You can add a small tenten (゛, two short dashes) to certain Hiragana characters to "voice" their consonant. This changes a hard, unvoiced sound (like 'k') into a softer, voiced sound (like 'g').

## Combined Sounds (Yōon 拗音)

Yōon are formed by combining an 'i'-vowel Hiragana from the 'ki,' 'shi,' 'chi,' 'ni,' 'hi,' 'mi,' or 'ri' rows (and their voiced/p-sound counterparts) with a smaller version of や (ya), ゆ (yu), or よ (yo).

The small や, ゆ, よ characters affect the preceding consonant-i sound, essentially merging them.
